# Producer Independence and Frontier Harvesting

## Doctrine
No external model, harvester, crawler, browser provider, or AI vendor is required for CivicLenZ continuity. HERMES owns research intent, work identity, priority, state, contracts, validation, monitoring, and recovery. Producers are replaceable executors.

Gemini/CivicsLenZz is a preferred high-productivity research producer, not a truth authority or a second control plane.

## Canonical ownership
HERMES Prime owns the Research Work Ledger. Every substantive task has a deterministic ResearchWorkIdentity based on subject, scope, applicable time/dataset period, and where needed source family. A ResearchReservation identifies the active producer and lease. Producers must not independently duplicate an active equivalent reservation.

Cloudflare, CivicsLenZz/Gemini, OpenClaw/browser workers, VPS workers, local Qwen, and future producers all execute work under compatible contracts. If a producer fails, its reservation expires/requeues and HERMES routes eligible work elsewhere.

No unique civic knowledge may exist only in a model conversation or producer-local context. Reproducible research must preserve source, retrieval time, method/parser/query where relevant, dataset period, result, negative-result scope, raw evidence, content hash, producer/version, and contract/version.

## Frontier model
HERMES operates the current canonical activation cohort while a harvester may prepare the next approved cohort.

Typical progression:
- CANONICAL_ACTIVE: HERMES validates, deepens, reconciles, publishes eligible claims, and monitors.
- NEXT_FRONTIER: preferred harvester discovers jurisdictions, Seat candidates, elections, candidates, source systems, GIS, parsers, initial dossiers, and raw evidence.
- FOLLOWING_FRONTIER: source/GIS reconnaissance when capacity permits.

Suggested cohort states:
DISCOVERING -> HARVESTING -> READY_FOR_HERMES_REVIEW -> ACCEPTED -> ACTIVATING -> CANONICAL_ACTIVE -> MONITORING.

All frontier outputs remain extracted_unreviewed until canonical validation.

## Cohort Readiness Package
A handoff should report at minimum:
- cohort identity and contract version;
- jurisdictions discovered and unresolved;
- Seat candidates and source evidence;
- election coverage per Seat;
- CandidateCampaign discoveries;
- current-occupant extractions;
- research-contract coverage/gaps;
- official/election/finance/GIS/source candidates;
- reusable parser/source families;
- raw evidence/retrieval counts;
- known source failures and unresolved GIS boundaries;
- duplicate/identity warnings;
- readiness state.

Physical counts must be reported as HARVESTED, EXTRACTED_UNREVIEWED, EXPORTED, CANONICAL_ACCEPTED, and VERIFIED. A producer cannot claim the last two without canonical HERMES acceptance/validation.

## Spare capacity
When no higher-priority HERMES assignment exists, approved frontier capacity may consume source discovery, GIS discovery, parser generalization, historical archives, campaign-site archiving, election history, source health, or Academy work. It must not duplicate active canonical reservations merely to keep compute busy.

## Delegation after handoff
When a cohort becomes CANONICAL_ACTIVE, autonomous frontier capacity advances. The harvester remains an eligible delegated producer: HERMES may assign a specific research gap back to it without surrendering canonical work ownership.

## Producer fallback
Capabilities may have preferred and fallback producers. Example: source discovery may prefer CivicsLenZz/Gemini and fall back to OpenClaw/browser; structured HTML may prefer Cloudflare and fall back to a VPS HTTP worker; messy text may prefer local Qwen and escalate only when policy permits. HERMES owns routing and failure recovery.

## Evolution
Producer telemetry feeds Academy: yield, latency, parser drift, source failures, rate limits, duplicates, validation rejection, browser/model fallback, and resource use. Academy should convert repeated general discovery into deterministic reusable adapters where possible. HERMES may evolve research methods but not silently alter truth, verification, or publication standards.
