# Seat, Election, and Candidate Parallel Research

## Invariant
SEAT DISCOVERY REQUIRES ELECTION DISCOVERY.

CivicLenZ must never become an incumbent directory with candidates bolted on later. The permanent Seat is the anchor. As soon as a Seat is discovered, research branches in parallel into current occupancy and the election/candidate lifecycle.

Conceptual graph:

Jurisdiction -> Seat -> SeatOccupancy -> Person

and

Seat -> Election -> CandidateCampaign -> Person

A candidate who wins remains the same Person and gains a new SeatOccupancy; research/evidence history is preserved.

## Immediate obligations after Seat discovery
For each discovered Seat, create/resolve work for:
1. jurisdiction and authoritative Seat definition;
2. current occupancy/vacancy/acting status;
3. current Person identity and applicable officeholder ResearchContract;
4. election authority and election-cycle discovery;
5. current/upcoming election, filing and qualification windows;
6. CandidateCampaign discovery/status for every discovered candidate;
7. Person identity resolution for each candidate;
8. applicable CandidateCampaign/Person research contract;
9. evidence/currentness/monitoring for all of the above.

A cohort cannot claim meaningful Seat readiness merely because Seat titles and current occupants were found. It must report election and candidate coverage/gaps for the same Seat universe.

## CandidateCampaign research
Candidate research is first-class and may include, as lawfully public and civically relevant:
- filing/qualification/withdrawal/status and party;
- election and Seat linkage;
- campaign committee and finance authority;
- campaign website and archived versions;
- official/campaign contact and social accounts;
- biography, education, career, prior offices/candidacies;
- campaign finance, filings, receipts/expenditures and reconciliation;
- required/public disclosures and business interests;
- platform, issue positions, promises, commitments, qualifiers/red lines;
- endorsements, debates/interviews, public statements and material campaign social activity;
- relevant ethics/public records with strong identity/evidence safeguards;
- election results/certification;
- source provenance, evidence, contradictions, currentness and monitoring.

Do not treat public candidacy as permission for indiscriminate private-life surveillance. Research must be lawfully public, identity-resolved, evidence-backed, and materially relevant to civic evaluation, public conduct, conflicts, campaign activity, governance, or accountability.

## Campaign promise lifecycle
Campaign websites and campaign materials are primary campaign sources, not independent verification of external factual claims. Preserve pages/snapshots and extract candidate commitments with date, context, qualifiers, source and evidence. Later statements/actions/votes may be linked to the promise graph. Strong conclusions require appropriate evidence; ambiguous rhetoric should not automatically become a broken-promise assertion.

## First-pass harvester
A frontier harvester that discovers a Seat should concurrently discover the current occupant, election system, election cycles, candidate filings, and first-pass research packages for both incumbent and candidates according to canonical contracts. It may enumerate/reconcile datasets where feasible. All outputs remain `extracted_unreviewed` until canonical validation.

## Canonical handoff
HERMES receives a Seat-centric package rather than disconnected politician records. It independently:
- resolves canonical Seat/Person/Election/CandidateCampaign identity;
- detects duplicates/reservations;
- validates source/evidence;
- corroborates claims when the contract requires it;
- reconciles finite datasets;
- fills research gaps;
- resolves contradictions;
- applies publication gates;
- establishes continuous monitoring.

## Monitoring
Election/candidate state is volatile. Filing status, qualification, withdrawal, finance, campaign platforms, statements, endorsements and results require cadence appropriate to election proximity and source volatility. Election-night/result monitoring may be much faster than background research. Currentness is expressed with cutoff/next-check state, never global completion.

## Handoff to occupancy
When an election result is sufficiently authoritative/certified under the applicable contract, HERMES may prepare the transition from CandidateCampaign to SeatOccupancy. The Person and all prior evidence remain continuous; do not generate a new politician identity merely because office status changed.

## Readiness reporting
For every cohort report at minimum:
- Seats discovered / expected where enumerable;
- occupancy coverage;
- election-authority coverage;
- current/upcoming election coverage;
- CandidateCampaigns discovered;
- filing/qualification reconciliation status;
- candidate dossier first-pass coverage;
- unresolved candidate identities/duplicates;
- source/evidence counts;
- monitoring readiness.

No synthetic candidate is ever created to make a race look populated.
