# Civic Territory & Public Resource Graph

## Purpose
A Seat governs/represents a civic territory, not merely a politician profile. CivicLenZ should connect versioned boundaries to sourced constituency, economic, government-finance, public-resource, project, community-condition, and oversight context.

## Territory intelligence
Attach metrics to boundary version + reference period + source/methodology, never timelessly to a politician. Domains may include population/households/voting-age context, public voter-registration statistics where lawful/authoritative, employment/income/housing/education/language, business establishments/industry/payroll, public safety, transportation, schools, environment, housing, and other civically relevant public datasets.

Do not mix incompatible vintages/geographies without explicit methodology.

## Public resource graph
Model money/resources as relationships rather than a single `money controlled` number:
Government Entity -> Program/Budget -> Revenue/Transfer/Appropriation/Award/Contract/Grant -> Recipient/Project -> Geography.

Track where supported:
- revenue and source categories;
- expenditures/functions;
- debt/assets/liabilities;
- federal/state/local intergovernmental transfers;
- grants/contracts/procurement;
- capital projects;
- audit/financial-report context.

## Attribution discipline
Separate:
1. GOVERNMENT FINANCIAL CONTEXT — what happens financially in a government/jurisdiction;
2. OFFICE AUTHORITY — what the Seat can lawfully propose/approve/vote/veto/administer/oversee;
3. OFFICIAL ACTION — what the occupant actually did.

Never imply that jurisdictional spending personally flowed through an official without evidence of that legal/transactional relationship.

## Logical directors
Constituency Intelligence Director: boundary-level people/economy/context.
Public Finance & Fiscal Flow Director: budgets, revenue, expenditures, transfers, grants, contracts, debt, projects, fiscal monitoring.
Community Conditions Director: sourced public-safety, housing, education, transportation and other community indicators.
Integrity & Oversight Director: audits, inspectors general, comptrollers, ethics/oversight and authoritative enforcement/public-record findings.

## Integrity language
An anomaly is not fraud. CivicLenZ may flag evidence-backed procurement/spending anomalies for review, but must not label a person/entity fraudulent unless a qualifying authoritative finding supports that claim and applicable publication rules are satisfied.

## Monitoring/events
New budgets, awards, transfers, contracts, debt, projects, audits and material community-dataset updates may create research work/Civic Events after validation.

## Map projection
Map modes may expose Representation, Elections, Population, Economy, Public Money, Projects, Public Safety, Education, Housing, and Oversight. Every displayed metric must retain source, reference period, geography/boundary version, methodology/currentness, and known limitations.
