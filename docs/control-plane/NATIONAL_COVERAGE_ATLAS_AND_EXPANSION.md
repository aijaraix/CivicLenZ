# National Coverage Atlas & Expansion

## Objective
Scale CivicLenZ to the full U.S. civic Seat universe through parallel national/state backbones and a methodical local frontier. Expansion must preserve Seat/election/candidate/GIS/evidence contracts and remain producer-independent.

## Parallel tracks
### National/statewide backbone
Establish high-level permanent Seat universes early where authoritative datasets make this efficient: President/Vice President, U.S. Senate, U.S. House, and state-by-state statewide executive/legislative structures. Do not assume every state has identical independently elected executive offices; discover actual legal structure.

### Deep geographic frontier
Florida remains the reference state. Complete structural/local coverage progressively, beginning South Florida core and extended South Florida/Southwest, then Central/North/Panhandle and statewide reconciliation. After Florida, default expansion proceeds along an East Coast/northward band and then progressively westward across U.S. bands. HERMES may reorder based on election urgency, source/platform reuse, user demand, partnerships, data availability, or operational efficiency.

## State bootstrap
For each new state, map statewide executive structure, legislature, federal delegation linkage, election authority, campaign-finance/disclosure/ethics sources, state GIS, county/equivalent structure, municipalities, school governance, special-district structure, and reusable source/parser families before uncontrolled mass harvesting.

## Coverage Atlas
Track geographic nodes/layers with honest states such as NOT_SCOUTED, SCOUTING, SOURCES_MAPPED, HARVESTING, READY_FOR_HERMES, CANONICAL_ACTIVE, MONITORING, and GAPS_PRESENT.

Each geography reports separately:
- federal/state backbone;
- county/local structural Seat coverage;
- occupancy coverage;
- Election/CandidateCampaign coverage;
- GIS/address-resolution coverage;
- research-contract first-pass coverage;
- evidence/validation/monitoring state.

Do not collapse these into one misleading completion percentage.

## Frontier conveyor
Multiple safe phases may coexist: HERMES deepens/validates the active canonical cohort; a harvester prepares the next cohort; source/GIS reconnaissance may begin on the following cohort. ResearchWorkIdentity/Reservations prevent duplication.

## Candidate invariant
Every Seat frontier includes election authority/cycle and CandidateCampaign discovery/monitoring. `No candidates found as of cutoff` is a monitored search outcome, not permanent absence.

## National learning
Academy should identify reusable legislative/election/GIS/CMS/vendor/parser families across states/jurisdictions and convert recurring general discovery into tested deterministic adapters. National scale should become cheaper/faster as reusable source families accumulate.
